# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 9 August 2026

## The short version

Little RPG runs on your device. Your save lives in your own device's storage
and is never uploaded: we cannot see it, and we cannot recover it.

One feature does leave your device, and you should know about it before you
play: the **leaderboard**. When you rebirth or awaken, the game sends a short
line to a database so the boards can rank you: a random id for your device, the
nickname you chose, your league, your deepest stage and your best weekly
sprint. No name, no email, no account, no location, nothing about your phone.
That is the whole of it, and the section *The leaderboard* below spells out
every field, when each one is sent, and how to have your row deleted.

The game is free. If a version of it sells gems, the payment is handled by the
app store and never by us. See *Purchases*.

You do not have to take this on faith. The game is readable source, with no
minification and no bundled tracker, and it is public. Every line that touches
a network lives in `src/net/leaderboard.js` and `src/store/billing.js`, and
nothing else in the project makes a request of any kind.

## What the game stores on your device

**The save**, one entry in your browser's `localStorage`, under the key
`little-rpg.save.v1`. It holds your progress:

- Stage, deepest stage, gold, kills
- Upgrade levels, character level and experience
- Talent, relic and soul trees, relics, souls, rebirths and awakenings
- Soul dust, forged gear, dungeon keys, gems and your best gold rate
- Gathering skills: levels, trees, tools, raw and refined resources
- Pets: which are tamed, their levels, their armor and who is out on a trip
- Potion and dish timers: seconds remaining on active effects
- Lifetime counters used by feats and the bestiary: kills by species, bosses,
  deaths, forges, refines, feeds, brews, dungeon clears
- Settings: buy-max, auto-forge, sound, music, damage numbers and language
- **The nickname you typed for the leaderboard**, if you typed one, so it
  travels with an exported save
- A timestamp of when you last had the game open, used only to work out
  offline earnings the next time you open it
- If your copy sells gems: the store's receipt id for each gem pack already
  delivered, so an interrupted purchase is not paid out twice. The last fifty
  are kept

**A device id**, one entry under `little-rpg.device.v1`. It is a random UUID
generated by your browser the first time the leaderboard needs one. It is not
derived from your hardware, your account or anything about you, it is not an
advertising identifier, and it exists for exactly one reason: so the board can
tell that two submissions are the same player and keep one row instead of two.
It is deliberately kept **out of the save**, so exporting your progress does
not carry your board identity to another device.

**Two leaderboard scratch entries**, under `little-rpg.lbqueue.v1` (one
submission that failed to send, held until the next attempt) and
`little-rpg.lbcache.v1` (the last board you looked at, so it can be shown
again without a round trip).

Clearing site data for wherever you play removes all of the above.

The game reads exactly two things from your browser beyond that:
`window.devicePixelRatio`, so the pixel art renders sharply, and
`document.hidden`, so the fight keeps running while the tab is in the
background. Neither is stored and neither is sent. Sound is synthesized on
your device as it plays; there are no audio files and nothing is recorded.

Export save shows you the exact data leaving with you: it is the save
described above, encoded as one line of text, and it only goes where you
paste it.

## The leaderboard

The boards rank three leagues by deepest stage and by a weekly sprint. Taking
part is what the following paragraphs describe.

**What is sent.** Six fields, and nothing else:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| device id | the random UUID described above |
| nickname | the name you typed, or `Hero` if you never typed one |
| league | which of the three leagues your spending puts you in |
| deepest stage | the highest stage this save has reached |
| weekly sprint | your best stage within the current week's window |
| week number | which week the sprint belongs to |

**When it is sent.** Automatically, when you **rebirth**, when you **awaken**,
when you **open the boards**, and when you **save a nickname**. It is not sent
while you are simply playing, and it is never sent from a copy running on
`localhost`.

**Where it goes.** To a database hosted by **Supabase**, run by us for this
game alone. The submission is a single call that stores the best value it has
seen for your device id; it cannot read anything else about you, because there
is nothing else there. Rows are kept while the board is running. Supabase, and
**Vercel**, which serves the game's files, will see the IP address your request
arrives from, as any web host does, and each is an independent controller of
its own server logs under its own policy.

**What is public.** Your nickname, your league and your stage numbers appear on
a board other players can read. Your device id is never shown, and there is no
way for another player to reach you.

**Choose your nickname accordingly.** It is free text that other people will
see. Do not put your real name, your email, your school, or anything you would
not paste into a public page.

**How to be removed.** Write to the address at the bottom of this page with the
nickname you used, and we will delete the row. If you would rather not appear
at all, do not save a nickname and do not rebirth while online, or play the
downloaded single file, which never contacts anything.

The whole board is best-effort fun, not a record of anything. If the database
ever goes away, the game keeps working exactly as it does now.

## What the game does not do

- No accounts, logins or profiles
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics
- No advertising, ad networks or advertising identifiers
- No cookies
- No third-party scripts, fonts, or content delivery networks. The game uses
  the monospace font already installed on your system
- No chat, no messages, no way for one player to contact another
- No selling or sharing of personal information with anyone
- No requests of any kind beyond the leaderboard described above and a gem
  purchase you started. Switch the network off and the game still plays: it is
  installable and fully offline, and only the boards go quiet

## Purchases

Little RPG is free to download and free to play. The only thing it ever sells
is gems, and gems can also be earned by playing. Payment is handled entirely
by the app store. **We never see or receive your card number, billing address
or payment credentials.**

The **pouch button** at the top right opens the store. If your copy has a
store attached, it lists gem packs with prices in your own currency; if it
does not, it says so, and that copy sells nothing and makes no purchase of any
kind. The version on the web has no store attached.

Where a store does exist, the purchase is carried out **by the app store's own
billing service**. The game asks the store what the packs cost and tells it
which one you chose; the store handles everything else.

What the game keeps afterwards is a **purchase token** for each pack it has
credited, stored in your save. It is a pseudonymous receipt id issued by the
store, and it exists so an interrupted purchase can be re-delivered without
paying you twice. It contains no name, no email and no payment details.

A future version may send that token, with your device id and the pack id, to
a small service of ours whose only job is to ask Google whether the purchase
is genuine, so that a paying player is placed in the right league. It would
learn nothing else, and this policy will say so plainly before that version
ships.

The store is the party that collects and controls your purchase data. What it
collects, how long it keeps it and what rights you have over it are governed
by **that store's privacy policy**, not this one. Please read it.

What we receive from the store is limited to what it shows a developer:
typically aggregate sales figures and payout records, and in some cases a
pseudonymous purchase or refund identifier for support. We do not use any of
it to build a profile.

If you contact us for support and include your email or an order number, we
hold that message for as long as it takes to help you and no longer.

## Where you got the game

This policy covers the game itself. It does not cover the platform you
obtained it from or the connection you used to download it.

Any store or web host will see your IP address and request details simply
because that is how the internet delivers a file, and stores additionally
hold your account and purchase history. Those parties are independent
controllers of that data under their own policies.

## Your data, your control

- **Delete your progress from inside the game:** *Ascend* tab, *Awaken*, then
  *Erase everything and start over*.
- **Delete everything from the browser:** clear site data or local storage for
  wherever the game is running. This removes the save, the device id and the
  leaderboard scratch entries together.
- **Delete your leaderboard row:** write to us with the nickname you used.
- **Back up or move your progress:** the *gear* button at the top right, then
  *Export save*, which writes your whole save to a file; *Import save* reads
  it back anywhere. Your device id is not included, by design.

Saves are per-browser and per-origin. The downloaded single file opened from
your disk and the hosted copy on the web keep separate saves, and clearing
your browser data removes the save with it. **We cannot recover a lost save,
because we never had a copy**, and that includes gems, whether they were
cleared for or bought. Export is the only backup there is, and it is worth
using before you clear browser data or change devices.

## Children

The game has no chat, no messages and no advertising. It does have one piece
of user-supplied text, the leaderboard nickname, which other players can see.
A child should not use their real name for it, and the same warning is printed
next to the field.

Purchases, where a store is attached, are made through that store, which
applies its own age and parental controls.

## Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe), the LGPD (Brazil) and the CCPA
(California) give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal
data, and to object to its processing.

For your save, those rights are already in your hands: it is on your device,
we never receive it, and you can export or erase it yourself at any time.

For the leaderboard, we hold one row: the fields listed above, keyed to a
random device id. Write to the address below with the nickname you used and we
will show you that row, correct it or delete it. Because the id is random and
we hold nothing else, that nickname is the only way we can find it.

For purchase data, the store is the controller, and you exercise those rights
with the store.

## Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting or transmitting anything beyond what is written
here, this policy will be updated before that version ships. The date at the
top always reflects the current version.
